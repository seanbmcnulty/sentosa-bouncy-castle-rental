Rules

Please note.

By booking you agree to these hire terms. Plain English for a small DIY rental — not a substitute for legal advice.

Self-setup

We deliver and pick up only. You inflate, anchor, supervise, and pack down. Adult supervision is required whenever children are on or near the castle.

Your responsibility

You use the castle at your own risk. You are responsible for safe setup on suitable ground, correct anchoring, blower use, and pack-down. We are not liable for injury, property damage, or party disruption arising from use or setup.

Delivery

Free on Sentosa. S$60 delivery & pickup elsewhere in Singapore. Restricted or offshore venues quoted case-by-case.

Power & space

13A power point within reach of the blower, and about 5.5m × 5.5m clear flat ground (3.2m+ ceiling indoors).

Weather

Do not inflate or use in rain or strong wind. Pack down dry. If returned wet or muddy we may charge a drying/cleaning fee. Weather cancellations: contact us early — we’ll try to rebook; paid fees are otherwise non-refundable unless we cancel.

Care & damage

No shoes, food, drink, or sharp objects on the castle. Normal wear is fine. Tears, burns, cigarette damage, lost blower, or serious staining may be charged at repair/replacement cost. Soft play balls are not included for now — bring your own if you want the pit filled.

Booking & payment

Full-day hires only. PayNow at booking. A slot is held as pending until we confirm payment and availability on WhatsApp. One hire per calendar day. If the date is already taken we’ll refund or move you.
