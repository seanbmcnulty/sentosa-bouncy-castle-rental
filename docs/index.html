13A power point within reach of the blower, and about 5.5m × 5.5m clear flat ground (3.2m+ ceiling indoors).

Weather

Do not inflate or use in rain or strong wind. Pack down dry. If returned wet or muddy we may charge a drying/cleaning fee. Weather cancellations: contact us early — we’ll try to rebook; paid fees are otherwise non-refundable unless we cancel.

Care & damage

No shoes, food, drink, or sharp objects on the castle. Normal wear is fine. Tears, burns, cigarette damage, lost blower, or serious staining may be charged at repair/replacement cost. Soft play balls are not included for now — bring your own if you want the pit filled.

FAQ

Bouncy castle rental questions.

Quick answers on hiring our white bouncy castle for a birthday party on Sentosa or anywhere in Singapore.

Where do you deliver the bouncy castle?

Delivery and pick-up are free anywhere on Sentosa. You can also collect and return it yourself for free at The Residences at W Singapore Sentosa Cove. For delivery and pick-up elsewhere in Singapore, add $60.

How much does it cost, and how long is the hire?

It's $129 for a full day. Need it longer? Extra consecutive days are $89 each. Just WhatsApp Yumi to arrange it. We take one hire per calendar day, and there's no deposit. You're only charged if the castle is torn, stained or lost.

Do you set it up for us?

No, it's self-setup. We deliver and collect (or you pick up). You inflate it with the included electric blower, anchor it, supervise the kids and pack it down. An adult must supervise whenever children are on or near the castle.

How big is it, and how much space do I need?

The castle is about 4.3m × 4.5m × 3.0m inflated (roughly 14 × 14.9 × 10 ft), roughly twice the floor area of a typical flat-sized rental castle. Allow about 5.5m × 5.5m of clear, flat ground. Indoors, you need a ceiling of 3.2m or more, so it suits lawns, patios and condo or hotel function rooms.

What power does it need?

The blower plugs into a standard 13A power point. Make sure there's one within reach of where the castle will stand.

What happens if it rains?

Don't inflate or use the castle in rain or strong wind, and pack it down dry. If the weather turns, contact us early and we'll try to rebook your date.

What's included?

You get the all-white bounce house with side slide and full-width front ball pit, plus the electric blower. Ball-pit balls aren't included, so bring your own if you want the pit filled. On-site setup and staff aren't included either.
